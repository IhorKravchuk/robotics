A plastic pipe with an internal diameter of 120 mm was used as the main body of the hull.

![Plastic hull tube with internal cooling fan]({{ '/assets/images/2009-10-12-roboyacht-hull-and-main-components/pic058.JPG' | relative_url }})

A fan was installed inside the tube to circulate air through the hull and cool heat-generating components such as the power supply and CPU.
The front section of the tube was cut at an angle to form a tapered nose cone, which houses an industrial single-board computer based on the ALi100 chipset and an AMD K6-500 CPU.

![Cutaway nose section for the onboard computer]({{ '/assets/images/2009-10-12-roboyacht-hull-and-main-components/pic028.JPG' | relative_url }})

In the middle section of the hull, the rotation drive for the wing mast (central spar) was installed. Due to the high mechanical loads, a worm gearbox driven by a 24V DC motor was selected.

![Worm gear drive mounted in the middle section]({{ '/assets/images/2009-10-12-roboyacht-hull-and-main-components/pic052.JPG' | relative_url }})

![Mast drive mount detail]({{ '/assets/images/2009-10-12-roboyacht-hull-and-main-components/pic047.JPG' | relative_url }})

A completely standard 600VA UPS was chosen as the power source; as subsequent tests showed, it provided enough energy for about an hour of autonomous operation for all onboard systems.

![600VA UPS battery and inverter assembly]({{ '/assets/images/2009-10-12-roboyacht-hull-and-main-components/pic060.JPG' | relative_url }})
