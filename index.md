# Robotics Projects Archive (2004–2012)

Welcome to the archive of my early robotics projects, migrated from [robotics-ingvar-ua.blogspot.com](https://robotics-ingvar-ua.blogspot.com/).

This blog chronicles the evolution from early PC-controlled RC prototypes interfaced via an LPT port to the design, construction, and field testing of **Roboyacht (RYacht-1)**—an autonomous, wind-driven land/ice trimaran powered by a rigid wing sail and an onboard single-board computer.
