Sigh. It's been a while since I last worked on the robot. Right before winter set in, I decided to redesign the mast rotation assembly to make transportation easier, so I disassembled the vehicle into major subassemblies. I finished all the modifications, but wasn't in a rush to put it all back together. Winter—strangely enough :-)—turned out cold and snowy despite all the talk of global and local warming. I was much more concerned with keeping my own footing on the icy roads of our "cosmic" mayor than with testing the robot. A slight paradox: a robot designed for extreme conditions spent those very conditions waiting it out in the warmth of home :)

Now that winter and laziness have passed, I'm ready for action. I bought wheels—we're switching footwear for summer trials! The plan: final assembly, final tests, and out into the wild!
