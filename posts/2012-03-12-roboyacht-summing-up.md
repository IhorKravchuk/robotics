Before starting the RYacht-2 project, I decided to sum up what had been accomplished during the RYacht-1 project.

1. The project started with the idea of creating an unmanned, wind-driven trimaran for patrolling and exploration.
The main goal was to build an unmanned, eco-friendly vessel with a long range and the ability to operate autonomously for days or even weeks.

First sketches:

![Concept sketch 1](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/model01.jpg)

![Concept sketch 2](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/model02.jpg)

![Concept sketch 3](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/model04.jpg)

2. A wing sail was chosen as the propulsion system. This type of sail is easy to control by computer, requires a minimum number of servos, offers great aerodynamic performance, and provides surface area that can be used for solar panels.
So: wind power for motion, and solar power for the electronics and servos—plus batteries for backup and cloudy days :-)

3. The main hull was made from a plastic tube.

![Main hull plastic tube](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic003.JPG)

The "brain" is a single-board computer with an AMD K6-266 CPU mounted in the nose of the yacht.

![Single-board computer in the nose](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic020.JPG)

![Onboard computer and servo controller](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/PB300027.JPG)

The power supply and battery (UPS) were located in the center and rear sections of the hull.

![Power supply and battery placement](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic040.JPG)

![Rear view of power supply and cooling fan](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic041.JPG)

The hull housed two servos: a main one for wing-sail orientation and a smaller one for steering.

![Wing-sail orientation drive](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic002.JPG)

![Worm gear drive inside hull](../assets/images/2012-03-12-roboyacht-summing-up/pic054.JPG)
